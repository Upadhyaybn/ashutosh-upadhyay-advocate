## Production Requirements

The production architecture should prioritize:

- Low cost
- Reliability
- Security
- HTTPS
- Automated deployment
- Managed PostgreSQL
- Monitoring
- Backup strategy
- Client ownership of important production accounts

Cloudflare is the preferred DNS and edge platform, subject to
final production architecture.

Hosting will be finalized after the MVP works locally.
